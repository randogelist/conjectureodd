SHARED-SEQUENCE INDUCTION -- EXACT CONDITIONAL INPUT

Choose a profile family P(k)=(m_k,a_k,b_k), with m_k>=1, 1<=a_k<b_k,
and P(2)=(1,1,2). Choose one strictly increasing map sigma:N->N.
All weights use the same sampled paper parameter t_j=sigma(j)+1.
Lean j starts at zero; the paper strength index is j+1.

Let E_(k,p)(n)=L_(k,p)(n)*R_(k,p)(n) be the actual positive canonical
primitive polynomial value, sampled at paper t=n+1. The required inputs are:

BASE: For every j>=0,
  E_(2,P(2))(sigma(j)) <= exp(-(j+1)*(sigma(j)+1)).

STEP: For every fixed k>=2, there are theta_k>0, C_k>=0 and J_k such that
for every j>=J_k,
  E_(k+1,P(k+1))(sigma(j))
    <= exp(C_k*(sigma(j)+1))*E_(k,P(k))(sigma(j))^theta_k.

The transfer may change profiles, but must compare these actual products
including canonical primitive normalization. It holds only at the common
sampled indices; no all-index transfer is assumed or deduced. All constants
and thresholds may depend on k.

Finite induction yields for each fixed k>=2 a bound
  E_(k,P(k))(sigma(j))
    <= exp((D_k-Theta_k*(j+1))*(sigma(j)+1)), eventually in j,
where Theta_k>0 and D_k>=0. This beats every fixed rational denominator
because the exact polynomial degree is (b_k-a_k)*(sigma(j)+1).
The source also derives the exact logarithmic invariant as a Filter.Tendsto
statement: -log(E_(k,P(k))(sigma(j)))/(sigma(j)+1) tends to +infinity.
This is a formal source declaration pending native compilation and audit.

The conditional endpoint supplies irrationality of every odd zeta value
from weight five upward. The final all-odd endpoint additionally takes
irrationality of zeta(3) as a separate explicit input.

Neither an actual successful base sequence nor actual step estimates are
proved by this package. No input is installed as an axiom. No quadratic
decay or original separated ProfileGap conclusion is claimed. The old
quadratic router remains preserved and is not weakened or rewritten.
