DERIVATION ROUTER: HOW TO SUPPLY A ROUTE

Scope: proof-consuming Lean source, pending native compilation and audit.
No successful base, adjacent transfer, or all-weight family is supplied.
DERIVATION_REVIEW.txt records the paper calculations and their limitations.

1. THE OBJECT BEING ROUTED

A node x consists of a positive weight index k and an admissible fixed
profile p=(m,a,b), with m>=1 and 1<=a<b. Its sequences use the same t=n+1:

  N=a*t, K=b*t, h=(b-a)*t,
  L_x(t) = absolute leading coefficient of the canonical primitive polynomial,
  R_x(t) = positive Gram determinant / absolute slope determinant,
  E_x(t) = L_x(t)*R_x(t) > 0.

The upstream exact identity identifies E with the primitive polynomial's
value at zeta(2*k+1). Its degree is h. These are the actual ZH objects;
substituting an independently normalized polynomial is not a route.

2. THREE WAYS TO SUPPLY STARTING DECAY

DecayRoute.product:
  Supply epsilon>0 and an eventual bound E_x(t)<=exp(-epsilon*t^2).

DecayRoute.separatedGap:
  Supply the actual ProfileGap: 0<=A<B, with eventual bounds
  L_x(t)<=exp(A*t^2) and R_x(t)<=exp(-B*t^2).
  Multiplication gives epsilon=B-A. Thresholds can be enlarged.

DecayRoute.height:
  This branch is restricted to k=2, p=(1,1,2). Supply BOTH
  BaseHeightInput and AnalyticBaseInput from ProfileInduction. The former
  asks for a height exponent A<delta, where
      delta=2*log(pi/2)+(9/2)*log(3).
  The latter asks for every eta>0 an eventual bound
      R(t)<=exp(-(delta-eta)*t^2).
  Taking eta=(delta-A)/2 yields positive product decay.
  The analytic paper derivation does not itself fill this Lean input.

DecayRoute.sound converts any supplied branch into QuadraticDecay.
irrational_of_decayRoute then discharges the existing irrationality criterion.

3. THREE WAYS TO SUPPLY A TRANSFER

TransferRoute.direct:
  For actual source s and destination d, supply theta>0, C>=0 and
  an eventual same-index inequality
      E_d(t)<=exp(C*t)*E_s(t)^theta.

TransferRoute.paired:
  Supply theta>0 shared by both factor estimates, and C_L,C_R>=0:
      L_d(t)<=exp(C_L*t)*L_s(t)^theta,
      R_d(t)<=exp(C_R*t)*R_s(t)^theta.
  The route multiplies them to give C=C_L+C_R. Different factor powers
  require an additional argument; they are not accepted as a paired route.

TransferRoute.compose:
  Supply an accepted route s->mid and another mid->d. If their parameters
  are (theta1,C1) and (theta2,C2), composition gives
      theta=theta1*theta2, C=C2+theta2*C1.
  The exact intermediate node is shared. No sampling index is changed.

If E_s(t)<=exp(-epsilon*t^2), any accepted transfer gives
      E_d(t)<=exp(C*t-theta*epsilon*t^2)
             <=exp(-(theta*epsilon/2)*t^2)
  once t>=2*C/(theta*epsilon) and all input thresholds hold.
quadraticDecay_of_routes and irrational_of_routes implement this implication.

4. AN ADJACENT STEP WITH A GEOMETRY CHANGE

To move from s=(k,p_k) to d=(k+1,p_(k+1)), one possible route is
  s -> mid=(k+1,p_k) -> d.

The first edge may use a same-geometry adjacent R comparison, but it still
needs the matching primitive L comparison (or a direct product estimate).
The second edge needs its own geometry comparison for the actual product
or both actual factors. It is not supplied by the first edge's estimate.
Both edges may instead be replaced by one direct product comparison.

The raw slope/determinant weight identities in Theorem_2_5_Weight_Change
do not supply primitive leading-coefficient control. Content cancellation
in primitive normalization must be addressed in any arithmetic leaf proof.

5. TWO WAYS TO SUPPLY ALL WEIGHTS

PerWeightRoutes:
  For every k>=2, supply some admissible profile and its DecayRoute.
  The profiles need not be linked by transfers.

InductionRoutes:
  Supply a profile family P with P(2)=(1,1,2), a DecayRoute at k=2,
  and for every k>=2 a TransferRoute from (k,P(k)) to (k+1,P(k+1)).
  The constants and positive decay exponents may depend on k. No uniform
  positive lower bound on the decay exponents across all k is required.

AllOddRoute accepts either family. The endpoint
  ZHPreprint.DerivationRouter.all_odd_irrational_of_route
also requires the separate irrationality of zeta(3), and concludes
  for every integer k>0, zeta(2*k+1) is irrational.
This is an implication from supplied evidence; it does not assert that an
AllOddRoute exists. Proving its existence remains the mathematical problem.

6. WHAT THIS ROUTER DOES NOT INFER

An unfilled input is OPEN, not a contradiction. A candidate with upper
height bound 44 and lower decay bound about 5.8469 is INCONCLUSIVE.
Only correctly directed estimates can certify or exclude a route.
Paper-only exclusions are advisory, and are not constructors or Lean axioms.
Product decay does not by itself recover the original separated ProfileGap.
The weaker all-denominator criterion is not treated as quadratic decay.

7. SOURCE AND CHECK BOUNDARIES

transfer, and composition of product transfers.

and conditional single-weight/all-weight endpoints.

and restricted axiom audit. It checks dependencies, not the truth of a
missing estimate. Its success is meaningful only after a native run.
